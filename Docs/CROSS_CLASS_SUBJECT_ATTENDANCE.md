# Attendance for a subject in another year

A student keeps their main class (for example Year 12) and can additionally attend a subject taught in another class (for example Year 11 Maths).

1. An administrator opens **Class Management** and opens the class teaching the repeated subject, such as **Year 11**.
2. Open its subject assignments and edit **Maths** using the pencil button.
3. Under **Additional attendance students**, search for the Year 12 student and tick their name.
4. Click **Update Assignment**.
5. The Maths teacher opens **Subject Attendance → Year 11 → Maths**. The student appears with **(Year 12)** beside their name and can be marked present, absent, late, or on a school trip.

The same additional students appear in the administrator's Subject Attendance marking view. Their initial status comes from their own daily register, unless a subject-specific mark already exists. Saving writes a subject-attendance record under the class teaching that subject. It does not change the student's main class, daily register, or gradebook enrolment.

This is a manual attendance enrolment, not an automatic decision based on a failing grade. Administrators can add students for retakes or other cross-class attendance needs and untick them when they stop attending. Other subjects do not inherit this attendance enrolment. Withdrawn students are excluded. No existing student records or enrolments are migrated automatically.
